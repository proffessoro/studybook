# Studybook

An open-source platform where leaders run learning groups and can see which members actually did what was asked.

## Language

### Groups and people

**Group**:
A learning community with its own members, content, tasks, feed, and settings.
_Avoid_: Community, circle, class

**Leader**:
The one member of a group who owns it and has full control, including members' contacts and proof.
_Avoid_: Owner, creator, admin

**Moderator**:
A member a leader has promoted to create content and moderate the feed, without access to contacts, proof, or the dashboard.
_Avoid_: Assistant, co-leader

**Member**:
A person who belongs to a group.
_Avoid_: Learner, student, user (when talking about a group)

**Late joiner**:
A member who joined after some tasks were already published. Those earlier tasks don't apply to them.

**Pseudo**:
The public nickname that other members see instead of a person's real name.
_Avoid_: Username, handle, display name

### Tasks and progress

**Task**:
Something members are asked to do, completed by submitting proof of a chosen type.
_Avoid_: Assignment, homework, challenge

**Proof**:
What a member hands in to complete a task: a checkbox tick, file, photo, link, or passed quiz.
_Avoid_: Evidence, deliverable

**Submission**:
The record of a member's latest proof for one task.

**Deadline**:
The optional moment after which a task is closed for good. It can't be reopened or moved once passed.

**Missed**:
The status of a task that applied to a member but wasn't completed before its deadline.

**Points**:
What a member earns by completing tasks, used to rank members on the points board.
_Avoid_: Score (a score is a quiz result), XP
